# Product mapping research

Reviewed 24 November 2026 SGT. The four approved markets contain 16 systems and 56 circuit blocks. Public FECS provided exact identities, categories and media. Manufacturer datasheets supplied electrical and mechanical evidence. Primary design references explain representative circuits and architecture-dependent cases.

Astra independently reviewed each block and retrieved the manufacturer sheet for all 13 published Series. The 43 blocks with public Series give conditional part-selection examples. The other 13 display a component category, its possible function and a Technical Support link. Exact device requirements determine design-in.

## 25 November market page update

The current application JSON under `src/data/applications/` reflects these changes. The research tables below record the earlier review.

- The Consumer audio supply adds the PNS3010T candidate where a separate low-voltage regulator is used. The Hearables converter shows L2 only for a very-light-load auxiliary rail. The UAV charger, camera and control circuits retain the PNS family example. The robotic cleaner AC-DC block shows ferrite beads for a documented low-voltage output branch. [Official Consumer application guide](https://www.superworld.com.sg/applications/consumer/)
- Printer Wi-Fi, security Wi-Fi and smart-meter wireless modules describe inductor guidance for an external RF matching network. Printer power-supply guidance covers input filtering and isolated conversion according to topology. [Official Industrial application guide](https://www.superworld.com.sg/applications/industrial/)
- Thermostat display and interface guidance limits the L2 examples to documented very-light-load rails. Coffee-machine transformer selection remains dependent on the power topology and insulation requirements. [Official Smart Home application guide](https://www.superworld.com.sg/applications/smart-home/)
- The live FECS API confirmed all 13 exact catalog names, families, and datasheet downloads. The preview provides 16 filtered bundles, 27 general Specification Search links, and 13 category-guidance blocks with Technical Support links.

## Engineering scope

- Receiver fit requires the full coil envelope, the paired controller and transmitter, the mounting gap and thermal assessment. The 100 kHz test point on a coil datasheet is a test condition.
- RF candidates require a specific frequency, network, SKU and measured in-band characteristics. Ferrite chip beads support a specified supply branch where the measured impedance and DC drop requirements are compatible.
- NFC and mains-transformer decisions require topology, electrical constraints and applicable mechanical or safety limits. These blocks expose Technical Support where an exact part cannot be justified.
- Technical review covers component options. Customer design verification remains dependent on the actual circuit, enclosure and test data.
